About Catherine Liu

Catherine Liu has been a medium for fourteen years, and she is best known for helping people who have never had a reading before. She remembers how strange and slightly frightening it felt to sit down with a medium for the first time, so she begins every session with a plain-language explanation of what is going to happen and what she needs from you. There is no jargon and no test.

Her warmth is not a technique. Catherine grew up in a large family that gathered every Sunday to cook and talk, and many of her sittings gravitate toward the same themes: relatives, recipes, small rituals, the family jokes that nobody else would recognise. She is especially gentle with questions about parents and grandparents, understands that traditions around remembrance differ from family to family, and is curious about yours.

First-time sitters often worry that they are doing it wrong. Catherine reassures them that there is no right way. You can stay silent, ask questions, or say nothing about how you feel. She will check in frequently and is happy to stop and explain whatever she is doing. If you decide halfway through that mediumship is not for you, she will not mind.

She also offers gentle grief support. She is not a therapist and says so, but she has sat with hundreds of grieving relatives and has built a list of trusted organisations she shares freely. She does not make claims about curing sorrow or foretelling events. She sees her role as that of a friendly guide who helps you ask the questions you came with and hear your own answers a little more clearly. Her articles on remembrance and family appear on her author page.

If you are nervous about booking, Catherine offers a short introductory chat before your session so you can ask how she works and decide whether she feels like the right fit. She keeps a box of paper and pens in the room for clients who want to write down what they remember, and she is happy for you to take your time over a pot of tea while you decide how you want to use the hour.

How a session with Catherine goes

  1. What to expect

    Catherine explains the format in plain language before anything else.

  2. Conversation

    She shares impressions and invites you to react or to say nothing.

  3. Next steps

    She suggests small rituals and trusted support organisations.

Before you book

  • Bring any questions, even small ones.
  • Have a cup of tea nearby.
  • Tell Catherine about any traditions that are meaningful to your family.

Questions to bring

  • I have never done this before. What should I say?
  • Is it normal to feel nothing during a session?
  • Can we talk about my family's traditions?

Mediumship is a spiritual practice, not a science, and no medium can guarantee contact with a particular person. Sessions are offered for comfort and reflection, are not a substitute for medical, legal, financial or mental-health care, and are for adults aged 18 and over.
