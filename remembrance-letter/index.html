Remembrance Letter

Choose a gentle voice, pick a few keepsakes and share one small memory. An AI writing tool will draft an unsent letter, a short reflection or a small ritual that you can keep, print or change. It never speaks for the person you miss.

Why a letter, and not a message

Many people find that writing to someone they have lost helps, even when nothing is sent and nothing comes back. A letter keeps the conversation on your side of the page: what you remember, what you wish you had said, what you are carrying today. This tool offers a starting draft built from your own keepsakes, so you can edit it until it sounds like you.

It will not claim that anyone is listening, watching or answering. Mediumship is a spiritual practice that cannot be guaranteed or proven, and software cannot do it at all. If you would like to sit with a real person, our mediums offer sessions at a gentle pace.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a message from someone who has died?

No. The Remembrance Letter is an AI writing tool. It cannot contact anyone, and it is built so that it never writes in the voice of the person you are remembering. Everything it produces is based only on the keepsakes and memory you choose.

Is it a reading by one of your mediums?

No. The mediums on this site do not see what you write. The tool only borrows the general tone of the medium you pick, such as plain, slow or warm, to write in. For a live session, choose a medium and book a time.

What are the three forms?

An unsent letter is written from you to the person you miss and never needs to be sent. Words to hold is a short reflection about what your keepsakes may carry. A small ritual is a few gentle steps to take in about fifteen minutes.

Is my writing stored?

Nothing is saved in your browser. What you choose is sent once to produce the page. Please leave out surnames, addresses and contact details.

I am not coping well. What should I do?

Please talk to someone. The panel next to the tool lists free support lines, and our bereavement support page has more. If you may be in danger, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line straight away.

Would you like to talk with a person?

Our mediums offer unhurried sessions. Browse the profiles and choose someone whose way of working feels right.
